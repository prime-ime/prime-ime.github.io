### Research focus

Industrial energy systems involve coupled variables, dynamic operation, and complex operating conditions. We study how process data and domain knowledge can inform models of system behavior.

- Dynamic modeling of industrial processes and energy systems
- Relationships among variables and representations of operating states
- Model applicability across operating conditions

### Connections

System modeling provides context and structural information for energy forecasting, as well as a physical basis for combining mechanisms with data.
