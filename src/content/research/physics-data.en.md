### Research focus

Physical mechanisms provide structural knowledge, while observations capture variation in real operation. We study how they can complement each other in model design and learning.

- Representing physical laws and domain knowledge in models
- Integrating mechanistic information with data-derived features
- Consistency and applicability of hybrid models

### Connections

This direction links industrial energy modeling with deep learning for forecasting, focusing on the relationship between models and the systems they describe.
