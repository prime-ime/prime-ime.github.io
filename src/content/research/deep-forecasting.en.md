### Research focus

Energy time series exhibit variation across multiple temporal scales. We study how deep learning can represent these patterns and use historical observations and related variables for forecasting.

- Temporal dependencies and multiscale representation learning
- Multivariate energy forecasting
- Forecasting models and evaluation under complex operating conditions

### Connections

Combining forecasting methods with industrial system knowledge creates opportunities to study the interaction between physical constraints and data-driven models.
