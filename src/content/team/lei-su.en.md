Lei Su leads PRIME Lab. His research covers industrial AI, computing–energy coordination, and modeling and sensing of multienergy systems.

## Education

- 2018–2023: Ph.D., Control Theory and Control Engineering, Dalian University of Technology.
- 2015–2018: Master's degree, Software Engineering, Dalian Maritime University.
- 2011–2015: Bachelor's degree, Network Engineering, Dalian Minzu University.
